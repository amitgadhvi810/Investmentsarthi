Understand the risk

What if a key person was suddenly no longer there?

An unexpected loss can affect customers, day-to-day work and financial commitments.

  • Lost customers or business income
  • Difficulty finding a suitable replacement
  • Ongoing loans and other expenses
  • Recruitment and training costs
  • Pressure on owners, partners and families

Key lesson: Business growth is important. Business continuity planning is equally important.

The core concept

What Is Key Person Insurance?

Key Person Insurance (also called Keyman Insurance) is life cover that can help a business manage financial pressure if an important person dies.

In a typical eligible arrangement, the business owns the policy, pays premiums and receives the death benefit, subject to policy terms. This money may help with expenses or finding a replacement.

Important: Not everyone is eligible. The policy and insurer determine the coverage, ownership and payout. This is life cover, not property or general business insurance.

Who may be essential?

Who could be a key person?

Think about the person your business would struggle to operate without. Eligibility depends on the person's role and the insurer.

Founder or owner

Key relationships, strategic decisions and leadership.

Director or executive

Core management and business continuity.

Specialist professional

Skills or qualifications that may be difficult to replace.

Critical employee

Essential customer, technical or revenue responsibilities.

Beyond one policy

Plan beyond one policy

Life cover is only one part of planning. Also consider emergency funds, business loans, succession and your family's financial needs.

Partnership and ownership succession arrangements may require separate legal documentation and appropriately structured life cover. These are not automatically included in a Key Person Insurance policy.

Tax awareness in India

Key Person Insurance: what about tax?

Tax treatment depends on the policy and circumstances. It should not be the only reason to buy cover.

Premiums

A qualifying business-purpose premium may be deductible, depending on the policy, business purpose and applicable tax rules. It is not automatic.

Policy proceeds

A Keyman policy payout is not automatically tax-free. Tax treatment depends on the recipient and the law applicable to the tax year.

Ownership and assignment

Who owns the policy, who receives the money and whether ownership changes can affect tax treatment.

Professional review

Ask a qualified chartered accountant to check your circumstances. No deduction or tax outcome is guaranteed.

This is general educational information, not tax advice. Tax rules and section numbers can change. Confirm the law applicable to your tax year and policy with a qualified chartered accountant.

Official tax information: Income Tax Department. Seek professional advice for your particular situation.
