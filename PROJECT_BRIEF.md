# Project Brief — Investment Sarthi

## Business and target customers
Investment Sarthi will offer insurance products for working professionals and business owners aged 18–45.

## Brand and design
- Brand name: Investment Sarthi
- Brand feel: Modern and premium
- Colour direction: Red and blue combination

## Launch products and pricing
- Launch with 4–10 insurance plans
- Annual premiums: ₹1,00,000–₹5,00,000

## Website pages
- Home
- Plans
- Plan details
- About
- Contact
- Online checkout

## Images
Use clean placeholders or illustrations. Real plan photos are not available.

## Payments and contact
- Accept online payments, including UPI
- Customer contact channel: WhatsApp

## Must-have features
- Plan comparison
- Clear coverage details

investmentsarthi.in
